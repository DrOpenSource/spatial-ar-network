## 🖨️ 2. Printing Physical Triggers (Cost: ₹150 – ₹200)

1. Export your marker image (with high contrast border and clear QR code).
2. Go to any local stationery / digital print shop (e.g. in your neighborhood, Nehru Place, or CP).
3. Ask for: **Waterproof Vinyl Matte Sticker Print** (A4 sheet fits 6 to 8 3x3-inch stickers).
4. Total cost: **₹100 – ₹180**.

---

## 🎥 3. Pre-Vis Satellite Video (Cost: ₹0)

1. Open your terminal in `gods-eye-view`:
   ```bash
   npm run dev
   ```
2. Navigate to `http://localhost:4173`.
3. Use the search bar to fly to `Qutub Minar` or `DLF Cyber City Gurgaon`.
4. Open OBS Studio (Free) on your computer.
5. Record a 10-second smooth camera sweep:
   * Start at orbital Earth view with live atmospheric clouds.
   * Swoop smoothly down through the sky into Delhi.
   * Lock camera directly onto the target coordinates.
6. Export as `satellite_zoom.mp4`.

## 📱 4. Field Filming Day (Cost: Metro Fare)

1. Travel to your chosen location during daylight (good lighting ensures instant camera tracking).
2. Stick 1–2 markers at an overlook point (e.g. a viewing railing at Qutub Minar, or an outdoor standee/pillar at CyberHub).
3. Have a friend open your WebAR URL on their phone (Chrome or Safari).
4. Camera Operator (You) films over their shoulder in **4K 60fps**:
   * Show them pointing their camera at the physical sticker.
   * Frame the real landmark in the background.
   * Show the 3D hologram erupting on their screen.
   * Capture their genuine reaction (*"Whoa, how is this running in Safari?"*).
